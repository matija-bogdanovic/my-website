About me

A developer who started young and never stopped building. Comfortable anywhere from a no-code canvas to a custom codebase.

Hi, I'm Matija, a software developer from Serbia. I built my first websites at 16 and turned it into a side hustle all through high school, working with clients on Upwork before I had even graduated.

Since then I've built sites in Webflow, Framer and Shopify, and custom apps with React and Node.js. As a freelancer I've built websites for Conquer AI and their enterprise clients, and I worked on-site as a Webflow developer at Flow Ninja.

I care about the details: pixel-perfect builds, smooth interactions and sites that load fast. Outside of work you'll find me at the gym or playing guitar.
